## Smooth pop-in at the draw distance

With Smooth distance fade on, distant walls and floors fade in out of the fog or the sky instead of appearing in blocks.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/69cb8370-19d1-4211-b2b3-b8c4f0cb81a2

## Slowdowns

The game should slow down less when many enemies are on screen, and the sound should stutter less with it. This is part of ongoing performance work that continues in the next releases.

Capsules recorded with 60 FPS gameplay in an earlier version no longer replay in step.

Reported in #47.

## Sound

| | 0.3.1 | 0.4.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sound in the game's queue | 180 ms | 45 ms |

Reported in #48.

## Language discs

- Briefings wait as long as the French, German or Japanese version does, up to 11.6 seconds instead of 9. Mission 4 in French is no longer cut.
- A movie's sound starts one frame later with a French or German disc, as on those versions.

## Held weapon

With multisampling on, no dark lines along the held weapon and other textured pictures.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a9db310e-66e1-45dc-88b9-b86f7ed95134

## Settings

- Fog shape and Draw distance are on Enhancements, under Smooth distance fade.
- Only Even and Original for now. The rest come in a later release.

## Detailed log

Adds the sound device, the sound queue every 10 seconds and the movies' sound and picture timing.
