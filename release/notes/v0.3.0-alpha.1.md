## Controls

- **Number keys pick weapons** is its own setting now, off at first, in both schemes. Turn it on in **Controls**.
- The number keys and the weapon and psionic wheels carry an EXPERIMENTAL tag.
- A baton you pick with a number key or on the wheel stays in hand. The game used to swap it for the best gun with ammunition.
- Two number keys pressed quickly during a weapon change pick the second weapon once the change ends. The first one no longer opens again.
- The psionic wheel stays shut until you find the first psionic.
- The mouse no longer turns the view while the game is paused or one of its lists is open.
