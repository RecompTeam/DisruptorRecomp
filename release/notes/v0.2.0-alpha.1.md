## New launcher

- **DisruptorRecomp-win-Setup.exe** installs the game for your Windows user, with shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu.
- **DisruptorRecomp-win-Portable.zip** plays without installing.
- By default the launcher looks for a new release at every start. It downloads only what changed when it can, checks each package by its SHA-256 and installs it when you press **Restart and update**.
- Saves, settings and save states live outside the program folder, so an update keeps them. **Bring saves...** copies the saves of an older copy.
- The home page shows art from your own disc, the state of the disc and what is new in this version.
- Settings sit in the side bar: Display, Enhancements, Controls, Audio, Experimental and Troubleshooting. Each page except Troubleshooting resets on its own.
- **Help** shows the whole player's guide inside the launcher.

Coming from 0.1.0-alpha.5 or older: install 0.2.0-alpha.1 once with the Setup or the ZIP, then press **Bring saves...**. Later releases arrive through the launcher.

## Linux

- **DisruptorRecomp.AppImage** for Linux x64 with glibc 2.35 or newer: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and later.
- The launcher updates the AppImage in place. Saves and settings are kept in `~/.local/share/DisruptorRecompData`.
- Tested under WSLg on Ubuntu 22.04 up to the save-name screen. A level has not been played on Linux yet.

## Controls

- Choose **Modern** (WASD, left click fires, right click uses psionics, side buttons choose) or **Classic** (the original keys).
- Each of the game's actions can be moved to another key or gamepad button on the launcher's **Controls** page. Click it, then press the new one. The fixed keys, such as Escape, the F keys and **B** for fast-forward, stay as they are.
- A gamepad plays beside the keyboard and mouse, with an adjustable stick dead zone.
- The launcher's Controls page lists what each fixed key does.

- World textures no longer jump a texel as the camera moves.
- Gap filling fills only empty space, so no rims hang off the edges of objects.
- Shadows follow the picture's shape by themselves, the shadow setting is gone.
- The held weapon sits between whole pixels in widescreen.
- A state loaded mid-run no longer freezes the game.
- A number key you bind to an action no longer also picks a weapon.
- A wheel opens only on the key its choice is bound to. In the Modern scheme the Up and Down arrows step through it while you have not bound them to an action.

## Replay capsules

- The Debug tab lists your capsules with a picture each, and plays one under the mouse.
- **Ctrl+F12** records a capsule, **F12** loads the newest.
- A new **Texture coordinates** view shows how textures are laid on the world.
